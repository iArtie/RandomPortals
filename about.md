<cr>Random Portals</c>, as the name suggests, it makes every portal in the game COMPLETELY RANDOM!

Every time you load a level, <cr>all portals</c> are <cy>randomly replaced</c> with others. That way, even a simple layout can be fun to play, as long as it has a portal

To keep things from getting too chaotic, the mod includes <cg>4 wildcards</c>, each of which disables the randomization of the portal type you choose

Unfortunately, <cp>no two attempts are the same</c>, the more portals a level has, the less likely it is to have the perfect combination

The portals are completely random, but if you want a little hint, <cy>you can look at the particles to figure out which portal is coming next</c> ;)
